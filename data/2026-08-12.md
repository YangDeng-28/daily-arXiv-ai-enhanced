Method: 构建并运用一个AI研究系统（likely large-model-based）通过交互提示、搜索和启发式探索辅助证明构造与数值优化，结合传统分析技巧来推导并验证改进的上下界。

Result: 将已知界限分别改进到下界6π/11和上界π/(2log(1+√2))−10^{-4}，并提供了关于AI参与数学研究的经验总结，包括成功案例、失败模式、提示工程、验证流程及协同工作策略。

Conclusion: 在受控的研究流程与合适的数据/提示设计下，AI能够给出专家认为是新颖且有价值的数学见解，从而实际改进Grothendieck常数的已知界限，但AI并非全能，其发现需要专家验证与结构化交互。

Abstract: AI agents are increasingly used in mathematics research, but it is often unclear how to use them effectively. Towards this, we present an extensive case study of how AI was used to improve bounds on the Grothendieck constant $K_G$, which captures the hardness between combinatorial problems and their continuous relaxations. Specifically, while the precise value of $K_G$ is not known, we recently tightened the best known bounds to \[
  \frac{6π}{11}
  \;\le\;
  K_G
  \;\le\;
  \fracπ{2\log(1+\sqrt2)} - 10^{-4}. \] Crucially, these improvements were achieved using an AI research system that could arrive at insights deemed novel by domain experts. We give a detailed discussion of our experience using AI for mathematics research, particularly touching upon its strengths and weaknesses, as well as our experience with creating ideal conditions for AI to arrive at breakthrough insights.
