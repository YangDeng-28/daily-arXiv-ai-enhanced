### [1] [Regularized Emphatic Temporal-Difference Learning: Stability under Constant Stepsizes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19170)

Main category: cs.AI

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Address mismatch between expected-map stability of ETD and observed sampled dynamics at constant stepsize, providing a principled modification that repairs post-shock sampled behavior while preserving ETD fixed points and practical implementability.
